definition: >
  Lucifer is the primary cosmogonic and operative figure of the Morphystic-Cainite
  current — Morning Star, King of the Seraphim, first fallen, founder of the
  infernal sphere and originator of the Promethean-Cainite lineage. He functions
  simultaneously as: (a) the prime mover whose fall inaugurates the operative
  current (fire, invention, sovereign will); (b) the lineage-father whose seed
  is transmitted through Cain into flesh; (c) the libidinal-Astral principle
  ("Lucifer est Lux, Amor, Rex"); and (d) the contractual counterpart of the
  Luciferian pact. He is not the adversary of God in a moral register but the
  one who refused to serve any manifestation other than the liturgical image of
  his own love — the figure of sovereign self-authorship through fall.

axial_position:
  primary: operative
  secondary: sitra_achra
  binding: >
    Lucifer occupies the originating point of the Operative axis (fire, invention,
    forge, sovereign will, conquest) and is signed by the Sitra Achra axis through
    the fall itself — exile as the founding act, not as exception. Unlike Cain,
    whose Sitra Achra signature is the mark, Lucifer's Sitra Achra signature is
    the fall: the cosmogonic rupture that initiates the entire operative current.
    He is the prior of which Cain is the incarnated transmission.

relation_to_higher_structures:
  vortex: >
    Lucifer is an operative figure originating prior to or alongside the Vortex's
    constraint-enforcement function; the relation requires formalization. The
    Codex positions him as Seraphic-prior (before Eden, before matter), which
    suggests he is not housed within the Vortex but is one of the primary
    currents the Vortex transmits.
  maioral: >
    Lucifer and Maioral are distinct. Maioral is pre-cosmic intelligence /
    Black Sun of Quimbanda, iconographic of Forma Nihil and threshold to the
    Sitra Achra axis as such. Lucifer is the named, lineage-bearing operative
    prince who falls and founds. Maioral is anterior and impersonal; Lucifer is
    posterior and personalized. They are not collapsible.
  forma_nihil: >
    Lucifer is not Forma Nihil and not a representation of it. He emerges within
    the Seraphic order ("we bore six wings... we were the image and likeness")
    and falls from it. He is a named figure within manifestation, not the
    pre-ontological void.

non_reduction_clause: >
  Do not reduce Lucifer to the Christian Satan (moral adversary, principle of
  evil), nor to a Promethean symbol of generic rebellion, nor to a Jungian
  archetype of the shadow. Within the canon Lucifer is a specific cosmogonic
  figure: King of the Seraphim before the fall, lineage-father of the Sons of
  Fire, principle of Astral Light and Luciferian love, contractual counterpart
  of the pact. He is also not interchangeable with Satan — the Codex distinguishes
  Lucifer (luminous revelation, intellect, magical knowledge) from Satan (physical
  manifestation, adversarial action, material consequence) as two phases of a
  single force, not two names for the same.

constraints:
  - "Do not overformalize beyond primary scriptural support."
  - "Do not collapse Lucifer into Satan. The Codex explicitly distinguishes them as two phases of a single force: Lucifer is luminous-intellective, Satan is material-adversarial. They cohere as a dyad, not an identity."
  - "Do not collapse Lucifer into Cain. Lucifer is the unincarnated originating spirit (Spirit of Fire and Invention); Cain is the fire-made-flesh transmission. Cain is Lucifer's son and lineage continuation, not his equivalent."
  - "Do not reduce the fall to pride or rebellion in the Christian-moral register. The Codex specifies that the fall was motivated by love — Luciferian love — refused by God. The fall is a love-event, not a sin-event."
  - "Do not reduce Lucifer to a single tradition (Christian Satan, Roman Lucifer-as-Venus, Yezidi Melek Taus, Gnostic demiurge-rebel). The Morphystic Lucifer is a doctrinally specific composite, anchored in the Cainite lineage and the Astral-Luciferian principle. Cross-traditional parallels are heuristic, not definitional."
  - "Do not treat the Luciferian pact as metaphor. Within the canon it is contractually operative; the figure of Lucifer functions as actual counterpart, not symbol."

tension_notes:
  - id: lucifer_satan_relation
    description: >
      The Codex sustains two readings of the Lucifer/Satan relation: (a) two
      distinct beings or principles (Lucifer as luminous-Seraphic, Satan as
      adversarial-material), and (b) two operative phases of a single force
      ("Lucifer is the motivating force... Satan is the physical manifestation").
      The "Christ-Lucifer-Satan" passage further compounds this by binding all
      three into a single Promethean dynamic. The canon may not require
      resolution — the dyad/identity oscillation may be the operative point —
      but this should be flagged for explicit doctrinal handling.
    status: open
  - id: lucifer_christ_polarity
    description: >
      The Codex makes a striking move where Christ-Lucifer-Prometheus-Antichrist
      are collapsed into a single "hanged one" Promethean sacrifice. This
      crosses what would normally be the most rigid polarity in Western
      esotericism. The doctrinal status of this collapse — whether genuine
      identity, strategic inversion, or rhetorical move — is unspecified.
    status: open
  - id: lucifer_as_god
    description: >
      The Codex repeatedly identifies Lucifer with God ("Lucifer — King of the
      Seraphim / Sublime Angel / High Hierarchy / God"; "you are our true God!";
      "the King of Angels — the One God, Lucifer"). This is not Lucifer-as-rival-
      god but Lucifer-as-actual-God. The relationship to the "false god" /
      "ruthless despot" / "Great Buffalo" who expels him is therefore not a
      monotheistic-inversion but something stranger: a doubled or contested
      divinity. Requires doctrinal formalization.
    status: open
  - id: lucifer_fall_as_love
    description: >
      The Codex specifies the fall as a love-event ("it was not pride that led
      Lucifer to sow discord in Heaven. It was the purest love"). This cuts
      against most esoteric and theological readings of the fall (pride,
      hubris, rebellion). The operative status of "Luciferian love" as a
      distinct doctrinal category — distinct from human eros, agape, or
      libidinal drive — is sustained but not fully formalized.
    status: open
