definition: >
  Cain is a primary cosmological and doctrinal figure within the Morphystic canon,
  functioning as lineage anchor of the Sons of Fire, exile-marked prototype of
  sovereign separation, and operative emblem of the forge — the figure in whom
  fire, work, sacrifice-memory, and self-forged becoming are bound into a single
  initiatic trajectory. He is both the son of Lucifer (by lineage of fire) and
  the first who carries the celestial relic into flesh, transmuting exile into
  craft and the mark into signature.

  binding: >
    Cain occupies the membrane where the Operative axis (work, forging, conquest,
    constraint-integration) is signed by the Sitra Achra axis (exile, separation,
    mark, anti-form). The exile-mark is not punitive but the sovereign signature
    that licenses operative function outside the cosmogonic given.

non_reduction_clause: >
  Do not reduce Cain to historical-biographical literalism (Genesis-as-history),
  nor to a generic psychological symbol (Jungian shadow archetype) detached from
  scriptural lineage function. Cain is neither ancestor-as-fact nor metaphor-as-
  interiority; he is an operative figure whose lineage is transmitted through
  spirit and craft, not blood alone.

tension_notes:
  - id: cain_lineage_mode
    description: >
      The Codex runs two readings of Cainite descent simultaneously without
      explicit resolution: (a) biological-symbolic (Cain as ancestor whose blood
      flows in a caste), and (b) initiatic (Cain as name earned through the
      forge). Both are scripturally supported. The canon may not require
      resolution — the doubled reading may itself be the operative point.
    status: open
  - id: cain_abel_polarity
    description: >
      Abel functions as the suppressed term whose absence structures the
      lineage. He is named only as the slain, the "mere ancestor shrouded in
      weakness," yet the first sacrifice is "made with a part of his own blood."
      The doctrinal status of Abel — whether negation, complement, or
      structural remainder — is not fully developed in the primary text.
    status: open
  - id: cain_and_the_vortex
    description: >

      operative function, or a parallel Luciferian descent, is unresolved.
    status: open
