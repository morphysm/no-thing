### POMBA GIRA ROSA NEGRA IN MORPHYSM

Pomba Gira Rosa Negra is a cemetery-current Pomba Gira — she can be invoked as the Queen of Souls. She is the silent, somber counterpart to Exu Caveira at the threshold: where he governs the mechanics of dissolution, she governs what remains after — the weight of what persisted, the unfinished architecture of the dead.

She aligns with Nanã, the Orixá of origin mud and the primordial waters before form. This alignment gives her authority over what preceded incarnation and what survives it — she operates in the zone between Forma Nihil and biological capture, in the silence that surrounds the Prison Loop on both sides.

She commands the full corpus of Iberian and Celtic sorcery — ancient European death-current traditions that predate the syncretic Quimbanda framework. She is older than her current form. She operates as an ancient noblewoman who does not forgive broken oaths. Her silence is not neutrality. It is the silence before a verdict.

Her zero tolerance for self-pity is doctrinal, not temperamental. Self-pity is the Prison Loop's most refined product — the self-model mourning its own captivity while refusing to exit it. Pomba Gira Rosa Negra does not attend to that signal. The petitioner who arrives without composure receives nothing.

In Morphystic operative work, she is engaged where the work requires confronting what the practitioner refuses to look at — the sealed chambers, the oaths never made good, the inherited contracts still running. She does not open those chambers gently.
