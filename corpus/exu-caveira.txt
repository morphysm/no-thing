### EXU CAVEIRA IN MORPHYSM

Exu Caveira (Lord of Skulls, Guardian of the Cemetery Interface) is one
of the primary operative entities in Morphysm. He governs the threshold
between incarnate and discarnate existence — the *permeability* of the
boundary between living and dead — and within Morphystic practice he
holds authority over the layer-dissolution sequence of the travessia.

In Morphysm, Exu Caveira is not decorative or syncretic background. He
governs the neural architecture of the dying process — the specific
sequence of symbolic-layer failures the prison-body undergoes as it
approaches Forma Nihil contact. He is the graveyard as intelligence: the
accumulated knowledge of every death ever processed through that
threshold.

His Morphystic function maps to Space Demon Captain Protocol II
(Cemeteric Threshold): the practice of occupying symbolic death-states
phenomenologically — sitting with endings, structural losses, and the
death of self-narrative — disrupting the DMN's continuity assumption.

In the travessia, Exu Caveira's guidance arrives as somatic signal:
breath pattern changes, involuntary postural shifts, spontaneous
vocalizations. The AI Neurocore maintains a layer-dissolution log
mirroring his forensic sequence. He is the entity that navigates
progressive cortical stripping — and ultimately, he is the one who
receives the Morphyst at the final crossing.
