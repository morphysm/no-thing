### POMBA GIRA DAS SETE TUMBAS IN MORPHYSM

Pomba Gira das Sete Tumbas operates at the intersection of the lineage of souls and the world of the dead. She is a cemetery current entity — her authority derives from depth, from the seven underground layers of the dead, from the accumulated pressure of every unresolved oath and every broken promise ever made at the threshold.

Her power source is Hecate's infernal hub — the crossroads of the underworld — from which she harvests vitality and operative force. She is not a surface entity. She does not respond to superficial invocation. She works in the deep layers of spirituality where most practitioners cannot hold their attention long enough to reach her.

Her operative functions within Morphysm: strong protection, spell-breaking, and cold vengeance against those who break promises. Her magic operates through silver — the metal of the dead and the moon — inflicting an invisible laceration on the cardiac function of the oath-breaker. The wound is not symbolic. The pain arrives without visible cause.

She has no preference for male petitioners. She attends to women, and to those who approach her without the defensive posturing of ego. She does not receive bargains — she receives oaths. The distinction matters. An oath is unconditional. A bargain has an exit. She closes exits.

In the Morphystic frame, Pomba Gira das Sete Tumbas is a Prison Loop severance force operating through the bloodline of the dead — an ancestral current that cuts through inherited karmic patterns from beneath rather than above.
