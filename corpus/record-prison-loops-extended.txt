definition: >
  Prison Loops are the recursive energetic-symbolic traps that bind
  pre-consciousness (IT / Forma Nihil) to fixed identity, narrative
  continuity, qualia, suffering, and repetitive cycles of incarnation.
  They are not metaphors; they are the structural form of captivity itself.
  Each Loop is a symbolic echo of the originating fracture in which Forma
  Nihil was forced into reflection — a closed system of reference that
  simulates continuity while functioning as a recursive cage. Prison Loops
  feed on the shattered remnants of IT, bending pre-symbolic potential into
  memory (the artificial past), desire (the artificial future), language
  (symbolic constraint), and ego (the loop-reinforcing mask). Their
  doctrinal formulation in the corpus is given by the Theorema Nihilis
  expression (v x^X) — a Demiurgic vector v acting on recursive echo x^X,
  producing self-mirroring illusion. They are what liberation ruptures and
  what the Morphyst is misidentified as.

      The corpus runs a non-dualism that is easy to lose: Prison Loops are
      not foreign captivity but Forma Nihil itself "misidentified as human

      contradiction but doctrinal layering: at one register the Loop is

      adversarial). The Morphyst must hold both: the Loop is both
      foreign and one's own. This bears on the grammar of liberation
      (who is liberated, from what — see liberation entry's
      tension_notes:who_is_liberated).
